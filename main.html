Lonavala, Maharashtra


Lonavala is a beautiful hill station located in Maharashtra, approximately 65 km from Pune. It is famous for its scenic landscapes, lush green valleys, waterfalls, mountains and peaceful atmosphere. During the monsoon season, Lonavala becomes especially beautiful. It is an ideal destination for weekend trips, adventure activities and relaxation with friends and family.

Top 5 Attractions in Lonavala

  1. Tiger's Leap - A beautiful viewpoint offering breathtaking valley views.
  2. Bhushi Dam - A popular monsoon destination known for its flowing water and scenic surroundings.
  3. Lohagad Fort - A historic hill fort popular among trekking enthusiasts.
  4. Karla Caves - Ancient Buddhist rock-cut caves known for their historical architecture.
  5. Pawna Lake - A beautiful lakeside destination famous for camping and sunset views.

Must-Try Foods in Lonavala

  • Lonavala Chikki - A traditional sweet made with jaggery and peanuts.
  • Vada Pav - A popular Maharashtrian street food consisting of a spicy potato fritter in bread.
  • Misal Pav - A spicy Maharashtrian curry served with bread.
  • Hot Bhajiyas - Crispy fried snacks that are especially popular during monsoon.
  • Roasted Corn - Freshly roasted corn seasoned with lemon, salt and spices.
